TaskMetric AI — administrator-controlled MCDA profile amendment

This is an approved product direction: criterion weights and their distribution are determined by authorized system administrators/evaluation managers, NEVER autonomously by AI. The AI may propose task-specific criterion scores and explanations, but cannot change the weight policy. Profiles, rubric anchors, complexity bands, and optional story-point mappings must be versioned and configurable.
